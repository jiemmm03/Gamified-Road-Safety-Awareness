auto_awesome AI TUTOR RESPONSE · PHILIPPINE TRAFFIC RULE EXPLANATION
Answer: At an uncontrolled intersection without traffic lights or signs in the Philippines, the vehicle approaching from the right has legal right-of-way (R.A. 4136).

Why: Standardizing priority removes confusion and prevents conflicting maneuvers that cause right-angle and broadside collisions.

Safety Tip: Always decelerate, look left-right-left, and prepare to stop. Defensive drivers prioritize safety and courtesy over asserting technical priority.
